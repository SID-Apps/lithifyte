Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 August 2026

The short version: we never store your statements, transactions, balances or merchants. There is no finance backend that could receive them. Asking the optional hosted AI sends the slice that question needs to a model provider for that request — not the ledger, and we do not keep the prompt. Commands-only and a local model send nothing. On the hosted app we store your email for sign-in, and may record privacy-safe product usage events (for example “opened budgets”, not “spent €42 at a shop”). Self-hosted copies send nothing.

Who we are

Lithifyte is a household finance dashboard built by SID Labs. The app runs at app.lithifyte.com, sign-in at access.lithifyte.com, and this marketing site at lithifyte.com. The source code is public at github.com/SID-Apps/lithifyte under the AGPL-3.0 licence, so every claim on this page can be verified by reading the code.

Data controller and contact. For questions about this policy, or to exercise any of the rights below, email signin@lithifyte.com. Lithifyte is operated from Ireland. If you are in the EU/EEA and are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to your national supervisory authority; in Ireland that is the Data Protection Commission.

Your financial data: never stored by us

Lithifyte is a client-only calculator. There is no finance database on our side — we never keep your ledger, and we could not reconstruct it from what we hold. All computation (categorising, budgets, forecasts, net worth) happens in your browser. Your data is kept in your browser's local storage (localStorage and IndexedDB) on your device, and in backup files you export yourself.

  • Bank statements you import as CSV, spreadsheet, or a PDF with selectable text are parsed locally in your browser and never uploaded.
  • If you choose to import a scan or a photo, that page image is sent to Lithifyte’s own GPU so a vision model can transcribe it. You acknowledge a written notice first (Photographing a bank statement). The image never goes to a third party, is not stored (it exists only for the seconds it takes to read), and the figures it produces are checked against the statement’s own totals before anything is imported. A till receipt uses the same path for shop, date and amount paid (Logging a receipt). If you have configured your own local model (Ollama, LM Studio), the photo stays on your device instead. Photograph with your phone on a computer shows a QR: the photo sits in a one-time slot for ten minutes so this tab can read it, then the slot is deleted. How-to: Capture your first transaction.
  • Optional encrypted backups use your browser's built-in Web Crypto; the passphrase never leaves your device.
  • The optional local AI (categoriser and scan reader) talks to a model you run. The optional hosted Lithifyte Pro — our own model — sends only the slice a question needs, after you consent, through a pass-through relay that does not store the prompt.
  • You can confirm all of this by watching your browser's network tab while using the app. A CSV import sends nothing; a consented scan sends a page image to our relay and nothing else.

What we do collect: identity and product usage (hosted only)

If you use the hosted product and sign in, we store:

  • Your email address, with timestamps of first sign-up, last sign-in link request, last sign-in, and last activity.
  • A session token (cookie lf_session) so you stay signed in for up to 30 days.
  • Privacy-safe product events from the hosted app only — allowlisted names such as sign-in, session start, onboarding step completed, section opened (e.g. map / budget), and actions like “CSV uploaded” or “backup exported”. Events carry short enums only (never amounts, merchants, descriptions, account names, or statement contents).
  • Optional Plus vault blob — an encrypted backup envelope you created in the browser. We store the ciphertext. We do not have the passphrase and cannot read the contents.
  • Plan and quota counters (free / trial / Plus, hosted-AI question count). No amounts.

Sign-in uses Google or magic links — we never ask for or store a password, and we never ask for bank credentials. Email and usage data are used to operate access, understand activation and retention, send product emails you opt into later, and improve the product. They are not sold. Downloaded or self-hosted copies of the app do not phone home.

Cookies

We set one first-party cookie: lf_session, an HttpOnly session cookie scoped to lithifyte.com, used to keep you signed in. There are no advertising cookies and no third-party analytics cookies.

Analytics and tracking

We do not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or fingerprinting. On app.lithifyte.com only, the app may send the allowlisted product events above to our Worker at access.lithifyte.com. That is first-party product analytics for retention and product decisions — not a finance data pipeline. The Engine repository describes the public allowlist; operator endpoints are not published there.

Optional money digest email

If you use Alerts → Email me a money digest, the app composes a newsletter-style summary in your browser (overview, reminders, upcoming bills, goal pace). When you click send:

  • The rendered text/HTML is POSTed to our Worker solely so we can deliver it via our mail provider (Resend) to the email address on your signed-in session.
  • We do not store the message body on our servers after send.
  • We may record that a digest was sent (a boolean / event count only) for product analytics.
  • You can choose to hide euro amounts in the email. You control what leaves the browser for that one send.

This is not a standing copy of your finances on our servers, and it is never computed server-side from your bank data.

Infrastructure

Our web pages and the sign-in service are served by Cloudflare. Like any host, Cloudflare processes standard connection logs (such as IP addresses) to deliver the site and defend it against abuse. The marketing pages load fonts from Google Fonts and a small script library from unpkg (a CDN); the finance app itself loads nothing external.

Data retention and deletion

Your email record is kept while you have access. To have it deleted, open an issue at github.com/SID-Apps/lithifyte/issues (or use the contact route in the repository's SECURITY.md) and we will remove it. Your financial data needs no deletion request — it is on your device, and the app's own erase all / delete account controls remove it locally.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with an updated date. The Engine and this site are open source; hosted identity and billing are not published in the Engine repository.
