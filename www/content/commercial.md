## Two different things

**Lithifyte Plus** is a subscription on [app.lithifyte.com](https://app.lithifyte.com). Households pay for hosted conveniences. The file you can download does not need it.

**A commercial licence** is a contract with SID Labs to use the Engine in a closed product. Banks, advisors, and anyone who cannot take AGPL obligations need this. Households do not.

## What stays free

Import, categorise, the money map, budgets, forecast, wealth, full export, Commands-only, and a model you run yourself (Ollama, LM Studio, any OpenAI-compatible URL). That is [the public repository](https://github.com/SID-Apps/lithifyte), AGPL-3.0-or-later.

## Lithifyte Plus — €9 / month or €90 / year

Start from the hosted app → Settings → Lithifyte Plus. Your own model is always free.

## Commercial licence of the Engine

If you want to white-label the calculator, embed it in an advisor portal, or host a modified copy without publishing your changes, email **signin@lithifyte.com** with who you are, what you will ship, and an approximate user count.

A commercial licence covers the Engine. It does not include the Lithifyte name, the official host, or anyone’s household data.
