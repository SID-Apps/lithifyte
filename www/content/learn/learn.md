Every guide here follows three rules. **Plain words** — if a term is unavoidable it gets defined where it appears. **Real arithmetic** — worked with actual numbers, so you can check it rather than trust it. **No advice** — these pages explain how things work; what you do with your money is yours to decide, and nobody here earns a commission on the outcome.

Start anywhere. If you are not sure where, start with [what a budget actually is](/learn/what-is-a-budget) — most of the others build on it.
